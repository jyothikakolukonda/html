SalarJung Museum

History

 The Salar Jung Museum in Hyderabad, Telangana, was established in 1951 to house the extensive collection of art objects amassed by Nawab Mir Yousuf Ali Khan, also known as Salar Jung III.
His passion for collecting led him to acquire items from around the world, including paintings, sculptures, furniture, and household glass.
After his death without direct heirs in 1949, the collection was gifted to the nation to establish the museum.
