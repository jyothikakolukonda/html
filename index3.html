Ramoji Film City

History

 Ramoji Film City, the world's largest integrated film city, was established in 1996 by Ramoji Rao and is located near Hyderabad, Telangana.
It was created with the vision of becoming a one-stop destination for filmmaking, offering a range of facilities from film sets to live shows.
The city is also a popular tourist attraction and has been recognized by the Guinness World Records.
