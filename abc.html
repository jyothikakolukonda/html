Charminar

History

 The Charminar, a prominent landmark in Hyderabad, India, was built in 1591 by Sultan Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah.
While various theories exist regarding its construction, the most popular belief is that it was erected to celebrate Hyderabad's victory over a deadly plague.
Another theory suggests it was built in honor of Queen Bhagmati, who later became Queen Hyder Mahal.
Some also believe it was constructed to acknowledge the four pillars of Islam.
